# 🏪 StoreLink

### Multi-store Business Management Platform

StoreLink is a web-based business management platform designed to support multiple types of businesses, including retail, restaurant, and service businesses.

The system provides centralized management of products, inventory, employees, sales, and store information.

## ✨ Features

* Multi-store management
* Product management
* Inventory management
* Employee management
* Role-based permissions
* Sales and order management
* Authentication and authorization
* Sales summary and reporting

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
